`py-raised` counts the inputs where the PYTHON raises rather than refusing
cleanly -- `cutil.drop_if` throws `ValueError` and no caller catches it --
where the Go refuses with one line. Those two cannot agree textually, so what
is required of them is the property that matters: both exit non-zero and
neither writes the file.
